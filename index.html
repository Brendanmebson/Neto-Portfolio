EdTech · Gamification

Pollenair: solving the retention problem

RoleChief Operations Officer

The challenge

Students would sign up, engage for a few days, then stop. The product was good, but engagement was inconsistent and threatened long-term viability.

Results

  • Improved user retention through behavioral design
  • Redesigned onboarding to cut time-to-value from days to minutes
  • Introduced gamification mechanics that build habit loops and intrinsic motivation
  • Secured investor interest through clear metrics and storytelling
  • Created a repeatable user engagement framework as a competitive advantage

I didn't learn operations from a textbook. I learned by building e-commerce systems at 2 AM, hiring the 50th person, and scaling products from zero to revenue. I've made the mistakes so you don't have to.

I don't measure success by meetings held or tasks completed. I measure it by revenue scaled, users retained, and teams empowered. Everything else is secondary.

The goal isn't to make you dependent on me. It's to build operational systems and playbooks that run smoothly long after our engagement ends. I work myself out of a job, because that's the point.

Pattern recognition
